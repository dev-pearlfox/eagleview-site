Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how PearlFox ("we", "our", or "us"), a technology company based in India, handles information in connection with the Eagle View desktop application ("Application", "Service").

Eagle View is a productivity widget that connects to your Microsoft Outlook or Google Gmail account to display a real-time summary of emails, calendar meetings, flagged tasks and, where your Microsoft work account allows it, Microsoft Teams chats — all processed locally on your device. You may optionally also connect Jira, Azure DevOps, and/or Slack for the same kind of at-a-glance summary of those accounts; each is entirely opt-in and none is contacted unless you explicitly connect it.

We aim to follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), and internationally recognised privacy principles including OECD Privacy Guidelines and ISO/IEC 29101. Compliance work on these, and on ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2, is in progress; Eagle View has not yet been independently audited or certified against any of them.

Plain-language summary: Eagle View reads data from your email, calendar, chat and task providers using your own credentials. This data is shown to you inside the app. It is never sent to PearlFox's servers. We do not sell or monetise your personal data, and we share licensing details only with the service providers listed in Section 5.

Write actions you can trigger

These actions are available directly from Eagle View's interface. Each one is performed on your connected account only when you click the corresponding button — never automatically, never in the background, and never without you initiating it:

  • Mark as read — marks a message as read on your account (Outlook/Gmail)
  • Flag / unflag (star) — flags or unflags a message for follow-up (Outlook/Gmail)
  • Delete — deletes a specific message from your account (Outlook/Gmail)
  • Complete — marks a flagged/to-do item as done (Outlook/Gmail)
  • Transition — changes a Jira issue's status (e.g. To Do → In Progress), if you connect Jira
  • Update state — changes an Azure DevOps work item's state, if you connect Azure DevOps
  • Mark chat as read — marks a Microsoft Teams chat as read on your account. The extra Teams permission this needs is requested only at that moment

Eagle View can read, mark as read, flag and delete mail. It does not send, reply to, forward or draft mail.

License data is purpose-limited. The email address, device hardware identifier and the other licensing details above are used only to issue and validate licenses, to handle payments and refunds, to check student eligibility, and to send you emails about your own license or trial (your key, and reminders before it expires). They are never used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Local Storage & Security

The email, calendar, chat and task content that Eagle View handles stays on your local device (licensing details are held on our licensing server, see Section 5). The following files are created on your device during normal operation:

FileLocationContents
eagle-view-settings.json macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/com.manojpro.eagleview/

Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.manojpro.eagleview\logs\		 Diagnostic log (see below). Plain text, not encrypted. Only the last 5 days are kept
Eagle View-viewer-*.html Same folder as the log A temporary page that shows the log in your browser when you open the Log Viewer. Deleted when Eagle View quits

Diagnostic log and Log Viewer

  • The log records technical events (for example "refresh finished", "sign-in cancelled", an error code) so problems can be diagnosed. It is written to avoid personal data. Email addresses and full web addresses are removed from every line automatically, and your operating-system user name in file paths is replaced with <user>. The app is not written to log meeting titles, message text, or Jira / Azure DevOps / Slack names and ids. Sign-in tokens are not written to it.
  • Eagle View does not upload or email the log. It stays on your device unless you choose to send it. The Log Viewer (Settings, or Debug Logs on the sign-in screen) opens it in your browser and has two buttons, Download as TXT and Download as HTML, so you can save a copy and send it to PearlFox support yourself.
  • The Log Viewer page shows your license email address, and the downloaded file's name contains it (or your operating-system user name if there is none), the app version and the date, for example EagleView_Log_v1.1.7_you@example.com_2026-10-12_15-38-27.txt. Check the name before you share it publicly.
  • Old lines are removed automatically: entries older than 5 days are deleted each time Eagle View starts and each time the Log Viewer is opened. Factory Reset erases the log and any saved viewer pages.
  • Log files written by older builds of Eagle View (before the October 12, 2026 update) may still contain data that newer builds no longer write, until those lines age out after 5 days or you use Factory Reset.

Security Measures

  • Version 1.1.7 and later: the Gmail sign-in token is stored in your operating system's secure storage (macOS Keychain; Windows Credential Manager); the Outlook, Jira, Azure DevOps and Slack tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to disk, and the key is held in that secure storage. Versions before 1.1.7 stored these tokens in the settings file without encryption. For Jira this is your site address, email and API token; you can revoke the API token at any time in your Atlassian account
  • All API communication uses TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS only)
  • The Application requests only the permissions listed in Section 2, and requests the Teams permissions only when you use the related feature
  • No telemetry, crash reporting, or usage analytics data is transmitted from the Application. The Application does make a few background requests, listed under "Other services" in Section 5; like any web request, they reveal your IP address to those servers
You are in control of the data on your device. Because your email, calendar, chat and task content stays on your device, you retain full control of it. You can delete all stored data at any time with Settings → Log Out and Factory Reset in the Application. Deleting the files above or uninstalling the Application removes the files but does not remove the Gmail sign-in token or the encryption key held in your operating system's secure storage; to remove them, delete the entries named "EagleView" in Keychain Access (macOS) or Credential Manager (Windows).

Your Rights

For the licensing records we hold (Sections 2 and 6), contact us at dev@pearlfox.io.

You can view all data the app accesses directly within Eagle View's interface.

Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

Use Settings → Log Out and Factory Reset to erase all locally stored data, including the entries in your operating system's secure storage.

Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18)

Use Settings → Log Out to sign out of Microsoft or Gmail, and the Disconnect button to remove Jira, Azure DevOps or Slack.

Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

Your settings file is a standard JSON file you may copy or transfer freely.

Right to Object (Art. 21)

Uninstall the Application to cease all data processing immediately.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

Contact your national supervisory authority (e.g., ICO, CNIL, BfDI).

Rights under CCPA/CPRA CCPA

Right to Know

This Policy fully describes the categories of personal information processed.

Use Factory Reset in the app, or delete the settings, notes and log files and the "EagleView" entries in Keychain Access / Credential Manager.

We do not sell personal information. No opt-out is required.

Exercising your privacy rights will not affect your access to Eagle View.

International Data Transfers

PearlFox is headquartered in India. Eagle View processes your email, calendar, chat and task content locally on your device and does not send it to our servers. The licensing details described in Sections 2 and 5 are stored by our licensing provider, Supabase, in Tokyo, Japan. Our other providers (Razorpay, Resend, Zoho and GitHub) process data in their own locations, which may be in other countries.

When Eagle View connects to Microsoft or Google APIs on your behalf, your data may be processed on servers operated by these providers in various countries. These transfers are governed by Microsoft's and Google's respective data transfer mechanisms (including Standard Contractual Clauses under GDPR).

For enterprise deployments subject to data residency requirements, please ensure your Microsoft or Google tenant is configured with the appropriate regional data boundary settings before deploying Eagle View.
